Second time in peru, went to the central peruvian andes and did a lot of hiking. Huarez is such a cool place to base yourself out of if you love the outdoors. 

Did the 8 day huayhuash circuit, which was crazy beautiful. The scale of the mountains there is absolutely mind blowing. 

Also summitted nevado pisco which required traversing glaciers and ice climbing at high altitude. Probably the hardest thing I've ever done but definitely worth it.
