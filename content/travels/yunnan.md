Added on a trip to yuan after visiting family in china. Decided to go to yunnan because its landscapes range from tropical forests to deep gorges and alpine peaks. It's also extremely culturally diverse with 25 ethnic groups. 

The northwest part of yunnan is basically tibet without actually being in tibet, and there are tons of buddhist monasteries and things of that nature. For this trip after I didn't go further north than yubeng which is already pretty deep in the mountains, but I'd love to go back and go overland from shangri-la to chengdu, directly over the tibetan plateau.
